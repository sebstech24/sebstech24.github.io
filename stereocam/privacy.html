Privacy Policy

StereoCam: 3D Photo & Video · last updated 20 October 2026

StereoCam is made by the developer named on its App Store page ("we" in this policy).

The short version

The app collects nothing. It keeps your photos and videos on your iPhone and never sends them to us. The app has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. The only outside service it talks to is Apple's App Store, for purchases. If you use iCloud Photos or iCloud Backup, Apple keeps copies of the photos StereoCam adds to your library, and of the app's files, in your own iCloud account. We can't see them.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera: to film with two rear cameras at the same moment and make 3D photos and videos. The phone's own depth reading is used, on the phone, to set the 3D and to warn when something is too close.
  • Microphone: asked the first time you choose Video, to record sound with your videos. Without it, videos are silent.
  • Photos (add only): every photo and video you take, and every copy you save, is added to your photo library. The app can't see the rest of your library.
  • Photos you pick: "Import from Photos" opens Apple's photo picker. The app receives only the spatial photos and videos you pick, makes 3D copies of them in its gallery and leaves the originals untouched.
  • Motion sensors: to know when the phone is held sideways, to draw the level line and to steady videos.
  • Battery, temperature and free storage: checked on the phone to warn you when the battery is low, the phone is hot or storage is nearly full.

All of this happens on your iPhone. Lining up the two pictures, the depth and the wiggle are all worked out on the phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What is kept on your iPhone

  • Your shots: each photo and video is kept inside the app for its gallery, with a small file of technical notes used to line up the 3D (camera settings, alignment, tilt and phone model). An imported item also notes the photo picker's ID, so the same item isn't imported twice.
  • Camera setup notes: one file describing your iPhone's cameras (phone model, camera formats, lens calibration and any camera errors), used to line up the 3D. It is replaced as you film and removed when you delete the app.
  • Your settings: picture shape, frame rate, view mode, wiggle speed, whether you've seen the tutorial, whether StereoCam Pro is unlocked, the lens calibration the app measured, and how many shots you've taken (for the first-use tips).
  • Free-shot counter: how many of the 4 free photos or videos you've used, and whether the StereoCam Pro screen has appeared. It is kept in the iPhone's keychain, so it stays when you delete and reinstall the app. It holds only those two numbers, isn't synced through iCloud Keychain and is never sent to us. Like the app's other files, it is part of your iPhone's encrypted backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a backup onto a new iPhone brings it back.
  • Share files: when you share, the app makes a new file, for example a wiggle video. It sits in the app's temporary folder and is removed the next time you open the gallery.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Nothing is uploaded: the app has no servers.
  • We never see your photos, videos or anything else on your phone.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the file goes only where you send it, through Apple's share sheet. After that, the app or person you sent it to decides what happens to it, under their own privacy policy.

Purchases

StereoCam Pro (monthly, yearly or Lifetime) is sold and handled by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Pro. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Other companies

The only other company involved is Apple: the App Store for purchases, plus the iOS features the app uses (camera, Photos, the photo picker, the share sheet). Apple's handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. No other company receives anything from the app.

If you turned on Share With App Developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple also gives us anonymous download and sales totals. None of this identifies you.

How long things are kept, and deleting them

  • Shots stay in the app until you delete them in the gallery (open one and tap the trash, or tap Select, pick several and tap the trash). Their technical notes are deleted with them. The gallery is part of StereoCam Pro; without Pro, deleting the app removes the shots kept in it.
  • Copies in your photo library stay until you delete them in the Photos app.
  • Deleting StereoCam removes everything it kept on the iPhone except the free-shot counter in the keychain. That is removed when the iPhone is erased (Settings › General › Transfer or Reset iPhone), unless you then restore a backup that has it.
  • Apart from emails you send us, we hold no data about you. To have an email deleted, just ask.

Changing your mind

Turn camera, microphone or Photos access off or on any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, Microphone or Photos. Without the camera StereoCam can't film; without the microphone videos are silent; without Photos your shots are kept in the app only.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write or attach. We use it only to answer you, never share it, and delete it once your question is settled, or sooner if you ask.
